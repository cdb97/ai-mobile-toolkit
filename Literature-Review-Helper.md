## Prompt
```text
Based on the problem statement and thesis statement, we have identified two fields that will form the basis of our literature review:

1. Task allocation criteria
2. Human-AI interaction patterns

I will now share the PDFs with you. For each source, please prepare the following four parts in your response:

1. **Field** – State which of the two fields the source belongs to
2. **Relevant extract** – The passage or section from the source that relates to field 1 or 2
3. **Paraphrased version** – A paraphrased rendition suitable for use in the final thesis
4. **Source** – Full bibliographic reference

Confirmed?
```
