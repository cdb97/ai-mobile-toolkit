## Prompt
```
Based on the problem statement and thesis statement, I have identified the following fields for my literature review:

1. XXX
2. XXX

I will now share the PDFs with you. For each source, please prepare the following four parts in your response:

1. **Field** – State which field the source belongs to
2. **Relevant extract** – The passage or section from the source that relates to one of the identified fields
3. **Paraphrased version** – A paraphrased rendition suitable for use in the final thesis
4. **Source** – Full bibliographic reference

Confirmed?
```
